Adventures in Fire Island

Welcome to our travel blog on fire island a beautiful and scenic destination away from the hustle and bustle of the ever busy city of New york.

It is really a place to remember and cherish with.The birds fling along the blue sky and the various animals in the national park and also the beautiful beachside activities like the torch lighting and ohh my the food was out of this world.
